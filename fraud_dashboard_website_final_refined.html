Executive Fraud Overview

These visuals focus on fraud rates, fraud losses, confirmed fraud patterns, and operational monitoring priorities for fraud and financial crime teams.

Fraud Exposure

Only 0.17% of transactions were fraudulent, demonstrating a highly imbalanced fraud detection environment where a small number of events can drive significant risk.

Loss Impact

Confirmed fraud losses exceeded 60K, highlighting the importance of balancing fraud detection performance with customer experience and operational efficiency.

Monitoring Priorities

Transaction amount and transaction timing emerged as key monitoring dimensions, supporting targeted fraud controls and investigation prioritisation.

Fraud Investigation Hub

Views designed for analyst review: where fraud occurs, what values are involved, and which indicators show the biggest fraud-vs-normal separation.

Recommended Fraud Controls

  • Velocity rules: flag repeated high-value transactions within short time windows.
  • Amount threshold monitoring: prioritise high-value outliers and unusual amount bands.
  • Behavioural anomaly checks: compare transaction timing and amount against expected customer behaviour.
  • Risk-based queue prioritisation: route higher-value and higher-risk indicators to manual review first.
  • Weekly fraud MI: track fraud rate, fraud loss value, fraud per 1,000 transactions, and review outcomes.
